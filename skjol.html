Skjól · Avon, Colorado

Between Beaver Creek and Vail, built around an easy mountain week.

Scandinavian-inspired residences minutes from both mountains: a heated pool and spas that run all year, a fitness and wellness floor, ski lockers, a 24/7 front desk, and a shuttle that takes you to the lifts. Whole Foods and the shops and restaurants of the Village at Avon are a short walk from the lobby.

The neighborhood

The Village at Avon, at your door.

Skjól sits in the new Village at Avon, with Whole Foods, restaurants and shops a short walk away, Nottingham Lake and the Eagle River minutes beyond, and Beaver Creek Village ten minutes up the road. Eagle County Regional Airport is about 25 miles west.
