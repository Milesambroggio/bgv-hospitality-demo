Rental & Homecare Program

Our on-site rental program is designed to maximize your property's potential through professional revenue management, full-service guest support and seamless daily operations delivered by a dedicated, resort-based team.

  • Rental agreement split: 60% owner / 40% manager
  • Complimentary homecare services
  • Imperial chalet suites: complimentary furnishings package with a signed agreement
  • Winter and summer availability at least one year in advance, at least 30 days in each peak season, minimum three-year term

Homecare Program (non-rental)

Year-round protection and oversight of your residence through regular inspections, preventative maintenance and rapid-response service from staff who know your property personally. Flat monthly fee, charged quarterly in advance, plus applicable program expenses.

  • Skjól: lock-off, one- and two-bedroom $250; three-bedroom $350; four-bedroom $400; five-bedroom $500 per month
  • Imperial: chalet suite $250; one- and two-bedroom $300; three-bedroom $350; four-bedroom $400; five-bedroom $500 per month
