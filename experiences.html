Experiences

The mountains, made easy.

Every stay comes with a team that already knows the valley. Ask the front desk and it is handled.

What we arrange

Lift tickets, ski school, guides, dinner, groceries before you land.

Ski and ride

Breckenridge at your door; Beaver Creek and Vail by shuttle from Skjól.

Summer in the high country

Hiking, fly fishing, biking and the valley's festival calendar.

Wellness

Pools, spas, sauna and steam, fitness, and rooms designed for sleep at altitude.

Dine in, or out

On-site dining at the Imperial; the Village at Avon steps from Skjól.

Winter

Three of Colorado's great mountains.

From the Imperial, Breckenridge Ski Resort begins at the door: five peaks, the highest lift in North America, and Main Street at the bottom of the hill. From Skjól, the shuttle runs to Beaver Creek and Vail, with Game Creek Bowl, Beaver Creek Village and both resorts' ski schools within a short drive. Ski valet and heated gear storage at both properties mean boots are warm in the morning.

Summer

Long days, cool nights.

Hiking and biking from both front doors, fly fishing on the Eagle and Blue rivers, golf at Eagle Vail and Beaver Creek, the Bravo! Vail music festival, and Breckenridge's summer calendar of concerts, markets and the International Festival of Arts. Our concierge team books guides, tee times and tables.

Wellness and dining

Recover well. Eat well.

Year-round heated pools and spas at both properties, sauna and steam, fitness floors and, at Skjól, oxygenated bedrooms for sleep at altitude. The Imperial's restaurant, bar and lounge are steps from your chalet; at Skjól, the Village at Avon's restaurants and Whole Foods are a short walk, and grocery delivery before arrival is a phone call away.

Plan a stay

BGV Hospitality operates the private residences at Skjól, Avon and the Imperial Hotel & Private Residences, Breckenridge.
