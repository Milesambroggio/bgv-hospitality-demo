Imperial Chalets · Breckenridge

Ski-in chalets with a five-star hotel behind them.

At the base of Peak 8, the Imperial pairs private European-style chalets with the services of a Leading Hotels of the World member: front desk and concierge, housekeeping and engineering on site, over 70,000 square feet of amenities, and slopeside ski valet for the walk from your door to the lift.

The setting

Peak 8, with Main Street minutes away.

The Imperial sits at the base of Peak 8 with ski-in, ski-out access to Breckenridge Ski Resort: 35 lifts, 187 runs and 2,908 acres of terrain. Cucumber Gulch, a 188-acre wildlife preserve, is next door; historic Main Street is a short ride down the hill; Denver International Airport is about two hours east.
