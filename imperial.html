Imperial Hotel & Private Residences · Breckenridge

Ski-in chalets with a hotel behind them.

At the base of Peak 8, the Imperial pairs private chalets with the services of a Leading Hotels of the World member: front desk and concierge, housekeeping and engineering on site, an outdoor pool and spa deck, restaurant and lounge, and a wellness floor for the end of a ski day.

Four and five bedroom chalets

The largest homes at the Imperial, built for multi-generational stays with room for everyone at dinner.

The setting

Peak 8, with Main Street minutes away.

The Imperial sits at the base of Peak 8 with ski-in access to Breckenridge Ski Resort. Historic Main Street, with its restaurants, galleries and shops, is a short shuttle ride down the hill, and Denver International Airport is about two hours east.
