About BGV

A Colorado developer and operator, four decades in.

Breckenridge Grand Vacations is a Colorado-based real estate developer and property manager, and the parent company of Grand Timber Lodge, Grand Lodge on Peak 7, Grand Colorado on Peak 8 and the Breck Inn. BGV Hospitality extends that operating experience to the private residences at Skjól and the Imperial, with a team recognized as a national Top Workplaces award winner.

How we operate

The same team, whether you are the owner or the guest.

On site, every day

BGV Hospitality is the sole on-site property manager at Skjól and the Imperial: association and building management, front desk, housekeeping and engineering under one roof.

Hotel standards

The Imperial is a member of The Leading Hotels of the World. Housekeeping, linen and guest service at both properties run to that standard.

Local reservations

Bookings are answered by a reservations team in Breckenridge seven days a week, backed by revenue management and distribution across the top travel sites.
